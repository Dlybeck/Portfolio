How it works

A partner project from spring 2024: a convolutional neural network trained on a custom dataset of NBA statistics. We built it in PyTorch, along with a similar version in TensorFlow.

In our testing it predicted wins and losses well, while the exact point differential was harder, as expected. It called the 2024 NBA Playoffs with only 2 mistakes, including several upsets.
