## Owner decisions: project-local review, 2026-09-06

This review belongs only to Portfolio. Do not install a general-purpose audit

The definitions of Real-World Coherence and Grounding Review live in

### Physical continuity and scene coherence

- Original's arriving paper covers the sticky note; it does not rewrite the
  sticky note's text. Judge the complete transition and both surfaces, not
  just whether the final frame looks plausible.
- Judge the whole composition: do the objects, background, controls, and
  connecting cues and opened content make sense together? A recognizable object is not sufficient
  if it has no credible relationship to its environment or the content.
- The owner likes Cloudscape's current sky. A mountain background is a question
  illustrating scene review, not an instruction to replace that sky.
- The Sky Observations notebook is rejected as an unconvincing fit. A notebook
  is not required. Fog rolling in and revealing text is an owner-suggested
  possibility, not an approved replacement or a requirement to simulate fog.
- Preserve creative latitude and the clean SVG language. Functional binding
  can establish a notebook's construction; stains and arbitrary creases do
  not establish why a notebook belongs in the scene. Realism does not require
  photorealism, extra decoration, or an exhaustive physical simulation.

### Connections are part of the scene

**The navigation system is exempt from redesign.** This is a theme, not a
website rework. Preserve the existing point-and-click tile navigation, spatial
layout rules and relative tile placement, Parent/Child relationships, routes,
input semantics, and document flow. No rearrangement, new traversal mechanic,
or replacement interaction may be introduced to make a metaphor work.

Only the navigation's presentation is open to theming: artwork, connector
appearance, and visual animation. A different visual effect must communicate
the same navigation state and leave the same destinations directly usable.

Arrows, lines, dots, endpoints, and their movement must pass the same grounding
review as tiles and Documents. Calling them navigation conventions does not
exempt their visual form. Original's chalk arrows and constellation-map lines
are owner-cited coherent examples, not templates to copy into every world.
Unexplained dotted connections in Clouds and Lily are explicit review cases.

Explore scene-appropriate ways to depict the existing connections; a generic line or arrow is not the
only allowable expression. Flies, frogs, and motion trails are speculative
owner examples, not selected designs or a requirement for a character. Any
such effect is presentation only: it cannot become something the Viewer must
follow, chase, wait for, or click instead of the existing destination tile.

Assess candidates by what the viewer actually sees, not the story the author
can tell about them. Renaming unexplained dots a flight path is not sufficient.
Prototype materially different ideas, inspect them within the moving scene,
and revise or discard weak attempts. Preserve clarity, reduced-motion usability,
and the clean SVG language rather than adding spectacle to justify a connector.

### Review checkpoints

Review the plan before implementation so a weak premise is challenged before
substantial work. Review the actual result before every voluntary stopping
point or completion handoff in theme work. Inspect desktop/phone renderings
and relevant transitions; planning reasoning is not evidence of execution.
Fix problems within the authorized task and review the revised result before
calling it finished. A failed review should inform another creative iteration,
not merely a new explanation of the same failed design. An interruption or genuine authority boundary can still
require stopping; record incomplete review rather than claim a pass.

rules; a project-local grounding-review skill; and deterministic checks for
measurable invariants, extending the existing test/capture tools. Automated
geometry checks support visual judgment and cannot certify scene coherence.
Validate the workflow against demonstrated failures and accepted designs.

The owner authorized the review-system goal, not website changes. Project

### Mechanical evidence and human/agent judgment

Use the project's Python environment with `requirements-dev.txt` installed.
For remote Chromium, set `PLAYWRIGHT_WS_ENDPOINT` and
`PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSER_HOST` for that environment; otherwise Playwright uses its
local installed browser. No credentials or hosted AI calls are needed.

```sh
python scripts/check_grounding.py run --output tests/results/grounding/review-001
python scripts/check_grounding.py verify tests/results/grounding/review-001/receipt.json
```

Choose a **new** output directory for every run. The command preserves prior
evidence and records each command, logs, JUnit coverage, source fingerprint,
and terminal status. It rejects failed/skipped/empty coverage, timeouts, stale
source, and missing or modified evidence. Per-group timeout defaults to five
minutes. Output files record progress without recurring AI status calls.
`mechanical_checks_passed` means only that these guards passed; it never means
the theme passed grounding. `verify` checks freshness, not aesthetics or proof
against a dishonest author forging all artifacts.

The runner covers existing material/fit, schema, navigation/motion, and Original
guards. Add targeted checks when a new defect is measurable; existing coverage
is not a promise that every future theme or navigation regression is covered.
Use `scripts/capture_grounding.py` for a repeatable private-preview evidence
set, then **open and inspect** its images. Source identity must be reconciled
with the running preview; a local file hash cannot prove a remote server's
checkout. A still image cannot prove intermediate motion or reduced-motion
behavior. A missing browser or inaccessible preview leaves visual review
unverified, even when the source tests pass.

```sh
python scripts/capture_grounding.py --origin http://127.0.0.1:51354 \
  --theme canonical --theme clouds --output tests/results/grounding/captures-001
```

Use the actual preview origin and affected theme IDs. This capture command
uses its own browser contexts, fails on theme fallback, and preserves previous
output directories. Its contact sheets cover Home, long copy, text/photo
documents, and forward/reverse motion. Capture time is approximate, recorded
per frame; for an exact occlusion instant, supplement with animation-specific
tests or controlled frame sampling. It does not inspect the images for you.

Keep one concise review checkpoint in the current task notes: planning versus
rendered phase, scope/source, inspected evidence, findings, verdict and next
action. Verdicts are `ready`, `revise`, or `unverified`, scoped to the work
actually inspected. Record separately whether the owner accepted the design.
Mechanical failure blocks a ready handoff; mechanical success alone cannot
grant one. Semantic/visual findings require review, not keyword tests that
pretend to recognize whether a notebook belongs in a scene.

Plan-only work uses a plan review, not pretend screenshots of an unbuilt idea.
Approved Original chalk and constellation cues remain positive controls;
unexplained cloud dots and the rejected Sky Observations notebook remain
negative cases. Do not turn these examples into blanket bans on dotted lines,
notebooks, or creative future worlds. The relationship must make sense in the
actual scene and respect current owner decisions.

## Rule for subsequent authoring and review

Ask what each mark represents, what surface it belongs to, and what should
occlude it. A variation axis is not valuable if its outputs violate the object.
Flat leaves and islands can have oblique/irregular outlines; planetary globes
do not become ovals to fit text. Navigation remains exempt from redesign;
the visual presentation of its connectors is subject to the owner's whole-scene
requirement above. Grounding is never permission to change navigation.

Keep the established clean SVG language. Physical coherence is not a request
for photorealism, exhaustive simulation, or more decoration.
