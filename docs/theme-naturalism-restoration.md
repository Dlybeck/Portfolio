# Theme Naturalism Restoration Goal

Status: **Approved goal contract — implementation complete; owner review pending**

This document is the source of truth for the restoration goal approved after
reviewing the first modular Theme Pack implementation. It supersedes the theme
dispositions and Canonical fidelity assumptions in `docs/theme-refinement.md`
where they conflict. The earlier document remains useful for the invariant
navigation, content-state, text, and motion contracts.

## Objective

Restore the natural, hand-made quality of Original Paper and make perceptible
organic variation a first-class capability of the modular Theme Pack system,
while preserving the successful alternate worlds and improving the specific
weaknesses found during owner review.

The result must remain declarative: an ordinary theme is added or changed by
editing its Theme Pack assets and data, not by adding theme-name branches to
runtime code.

## Confirmed diagnosis

The first modular pass represented variation as named factors but flattened
several independent visual channels that made Original Paper feel natural. At
the start of this restoration, Canonical had one shared rotation for base and
expanded states, omitted independent X/Y jitter and tape angle, approximated
the original paper shapes as generated SVGs, replaced the turbulent
chalkboard texture with regular gradients, and reduced the original chalk
connector width, wobble, directionality, and arrowhead treatment.

This is why automated combination counts passed while the rendered world still
felt sterile. Acceptance must measure visible behavior and fidelity, not only
the number of factor labels in `tiles.json`.

The initial red-capable diagnostic reported 141 missing Original Paper signals.
It now passes after restoration:

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/check_canonical_fidelity.py
```

The command must fail before restoration and pass afterward.

## Theme dispositions

### Canonical Paper — restore faithfully

- Restore the actual pre-conversion chalkboard texture and chalk connector
  character.
- Restore independent base rotation, expanded rotation, X/Y jitter, tape
  angle, paper material, rip or fold, ink, font, underline, and base/expanded
  identity.
- Treat commit `77f4e59` as evidence, not inspiration. Do not create another
  cleaner interpretation of the paper world.
- Implement the restored behavior through general Theme Pack capabilities;
  do not revive a parallel Canonical-only presentation engine.

### Planets and Constellations — preserve and refine

The world is accepted and should not be redesigned.

- Prevent unfocused titles such as `3D Printing` from breaking inside a word
  or leaving one letter on a third line.
- Replace the fixed-period radial-gradient star tiles with a deterministic,
  irregular starfield that does not reveal a grid.
- Preserve the planet artwork, palette, constellation concept, and Grow motion.

### Lily Pond — preserve

The world is accepted. Do not restyle it while performing general-system work.
Only compatibility changes required by the expanded declarative variation
contract are allowed, and those changes must be visually neutral.

### Island Chain — retain and simplify

- Preserve the accepted island-chain world, navigation metaphor, palette, and
  overall composition.
- Make individual landmasses cleaner and less visually messy. Reduce arbitrary
  interior marks before adding new decoration.
- Improve organic coastline and connector/background variation without
  turning the islands into planets or abstract interface shapes.

### Cloudscape — disable, do not patch

Cloudscape is rejected in its current form. Its repeated blurred ellipses,
generic rounded document panels, and circular cloud vocabulary do not form a
convincing real-world metaphor.

- Remove Cloudscape from enabled theme selection and unpinned random choice.
- Keep its files only if useful as dormant development material.
- A future Cloudscape must begin as a separately approved redesign; it is not
  part of this restoration goal.

## Modular naturalism contract

A Theme Pack must be able to declare the following without theme-specific
runtime code:

- independent base and focused-object transforms, including rotation and X/Y
  offset;
- deterministic per-location variation with independently salted channels so
  nearby objects do not accidentally share an obvious pattern;
- object-detail transforms such as tape angle when that detail belongs to the
  world;
- background art or texture whose visible repetition is either intentionally
  real-world or absent;
- connector style plus deterministic per-relationship variation in curvature,
  stroke character, direction markers, dash rhythm, and related parameters;
- motion preset selection plus bounded per-location variation in timing,
  rotation, scale, and offset while preserving the preset's semantics;
- readable title composition that prefers word boundaries and fails visibly
  during validation instead of silently splitting a word into fragments.

Deterministic does not mean uniform. A location should remain visually stable
during an exploration, but natural-looking independent channels should keep
the whole Neighborhood from appearing machine-stamped.

## Proof conditions

The goal is complete only when all of the following are demonstrated:

1. Canonical visual comparisons at desktop and phone show the original
   chalkboard, chalk arrows, paper materials, rips, folds, tape, underlines,
   and independent placement character restored against commit `77f4e59`.
2. `scripts/check_canonical_fidelity.py` passes and guards the specific
   naturalism signals lost during modularization.
3. The Theme Pack schema and validator reject packs missing the required
   transform/variation contract, and the stable engine consumes that contract
   without theme-name branches.
4. Planets and Constellations retains its accepted artwork; its real titles
   wrap only at word boundaries in at most two lines, and its starfield has no
   fixed visible grid.
5. Lily Pond remains visually unchanged except for neutral compatibility work.
6. Island Chain keeps its accepted overall direction while its landmasses and
   environmental variation read more naturally and less messily.
7. Cloudscape cannot be selected by viewers and cannot be chosen by unpinned
   random selection.
8. Parent/Child navigation, neighboring hit targets, fixed content states,
   document flow, reduced motion, and phone/desktop responsiveness remain
   intact.
9. Automated tests, pack validation, targeted visual checks, and a refreshed
   owner-review capture set pass. Automated checks supplement rather than
   replace the final owner visual review.
