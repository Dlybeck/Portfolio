# Theme Naturalism Restoration Goal

Status: **Approved goal contract — implementation complete; owner review pending**

This document is the source of truth for the restoration goal approved after
reviewing the first modular Theme Pack implementation. It supersedes the theme
dispositions and Canonical fidelity assumptions in `docs/theme-refinement.md`
where they conflict. The earlier document remains useful for the invariant
navigation, content-state, text, and motion contracts.

## Objective

Restore the natural, hand-made quality of Original Paper and make perceptible
organic variation a first-class capability of the modular Theme Pack system,
while preserving the successful alternate worlds and improving the specific
weaknesses found during owner review.

The result must remain declarative: an ordinary theme is added or changed by
editing its Theme Pack assets and data, not by adding theme-name branches to
runtime code.

## Confirmed diagnosis

The first modular pass represented variation as named factors but flattened
several independent visual channels that made Original Paper feel natural. At
the start of this restoration, Canonical had one shared rotation for base and
expanded states, omitted independent X/Y jitter and tape angle, approximated
the original paper shapes as generated SVGs, replaced the turbulent
chalkboard texture with regular gradients, and reduced the original chalk
connector width, wobble, directionality, and arrowhead treatment.

This is why automated combination counts passed while the rendered world still
felt sterile. Acceptance must measure visible behavior and fidelity, not only
the number of factor labels in `tiles.json`.

The initial red-capable diagnostic reported 141 missing Original Paper signals.
It now passes after restoration:

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/check_canonical_fidelity.py
```

The command must fail before restoration and pass afterward.

## Theme dispositions

### Canonical Paper — restore faithfully

- Restore the actual pre-conversion chalkboard texture and chalk connector
  character.
- Restore independent base rotation, expanded rotation, X/Y jitter, tape
  angle, paper material, rip or fold, ink, font, underline, and base/expanded
  identity.
- Treat commit `77f4e59` as evidence, not inspiration. Do not create another
  cleaner interpretation of the paper world.
- Implement the restored behavior through general Theme Pack capabilities;
  do not revive a parallel Canonical-only presentation engine.

Canonical opened-page fidelity is the complete composition, not an iframe-only
color check. Review the live click-open state at `davidlybeck.com` and compare
the outer ruled sheet and tape together with the inner canvas, content-box
panel geometry, 24px title hierarchy, native paragraph rhythm, controls,
links, and media/model treatment at both phone and desktop sizes. A white
canvas alone is not evidence of fidelity.

### Planets and Constellations — preserve and refine

The world is accepted and should not be redesigned.

- Prevent unfocused titles such as `3D Printing` from breaking inside a word
  or leaving one letter on a third line.
- Replace the fixed-period radial-gradient star tiles with a deterministic,
  irregular starfield that does not reveal a grid.
- Preserve the planet artwork, palette, constellation concept, and Grow motion.

### Lily Pond — preserve

The world is accepted. Do not restyle it while performing general-system work.
Only compatibility changes required by the expanded declarative variation
contract are allowed, and those changes must be visually neutral.

### Island Chain — retain and simplify

- Preserve the accepted island-chain world, navigation metaphor, palette, and
  overall composition.
- Make individual landmasses cleaner and less visually messy. Reduce arbitrary
  interior marks before adding new decoration.
- Improve organic coastline and connector/background variation without
  turning the islands into planets or abstract interface shapes.

### Cloudscape — disable, do not patch

Cloudscape is rejected in its current form. Its repeated blurred ellipses,
generic rounded document panels, and circular cloud vocabulary do not form a
convincing real-world metaphor.

- Remove Cloudscape from enabled theme selection and unpinned random choice.
- Keep its files only if useful as dormant development material.
- A future Cloudscape must begin as a separately approved redesign; it is not
  part of this restoration goal.

## Modular naturalism contract

A Theme Pack must be able to declare the following without theme-specific
runtime code:

- independent base and focused-object transforms, including rotation and X/Y
  offset;
- deterministic per-location variation with independently salted channels so
  nearby objects do not accidentally share an obvious pattern;
- object-detail transforms such as tape angle when that detail belongs to the
  world;
- background art or texture whose visible repetition is either intentionally
  real-world or absent;
- connector style plus deterministic per-relationship variation in curvature,
  stroke character, direction markers, dash rhythm, and related parameters;
- motion preset selection plus bounded per-location variation in timing,
  rotation, scale, and offset while preserving the preset's semantics;
- readable title composition that prefers word boundaries and fails visibly
  during validation instead of silently splitting a word into fragments.

Deterministic does not mean uniform. A location should remain visually stable
during an exploration, but natural-looking independent channels should keep
the whole Neighborhood from appearing machine-stamped.

Grounding does not mean maximizing literal detail. Theme Packs—and any future
theme generator—start with material and silhouette, then add details that
explain the object's identity, construction, or use. Functional cues such as a
notebook's binding rings are welcome realism. Generic coffee stains, arbitrary
creases, registration marks, and other unrelated surface clutter are not. The
shared visual language remains clean, solid-color, SVG-like art rather than
simulated photorealism or accumulated ornamental evidence.

### Owner correction: preserve Original literally

The first review of the restoration found two remaining Canonical-only
reinterpretations. They are regressions, not new design directions:

- The SVG compiler had added a visible fiber/stripe path to every sticky.
  That path is removed. The original handwritten title underlines remain, but
  their asset transform is centered to reproduce the old title-relative
  placement.
- The modular document tokens replaced `main`'s translucent letter content
  with an opaque gray panel, a hard black border, a black title, and desktop
  viewer padding on phones. Untouched `main` proves the exact contract: the
  cream ruled outer letter remains visible through a transparent iframe and
  translucent white sections with soft gray borders; the title remains blue;
  and phones use the original narrower paper margins. Canonical now reproduces
  those values through Theme Pack data.

The focused browser regressions are:

```bash
.venv/bin/pytest -q \
  tests/test_themes.py::test_original_paper_does_not_invent_sticky_stripes \
  tests/test_themes.py::test_original_paper_page_texture_is_not_doubled
```

Both tests were observed failing before the correction and passing afterward.
No Lily, Planets, or Islands presentation or asset was changed in this
correction.

### Owner correction: quiet Lily Pond water

Owner phone review found Lily Pond's water background visually busy and
repetitive. The cause was a fixed repeating-radial ripple layer combined with
two separately tiled dot layers and two ambient rings. Lily now keeps the
accepted pads, typography, connectors, Grow motion, documents, and navigation,
while the water uses only its depth gradient plus a deterministic SVG of
independently varied ripple events across the full movable world. A follow-up
review found the first sparse version left Home with no visible ripples, so the
world is now divided only for density guarantees: each viewport-sized sector
receives four independently placed, sized, tilted, and shaded events. The SVG
still contains no reusable pattern element, and the ambient rings remain
disabled.

The focused regression was observed failing before the correction and passing
afterward:

```bash

  tests/test_themes.py::test_lily_uses_balanced_pack_owned_water_without_a_tiled_ripple_pattern \
  tests/test_theme_packs.py::test_repository_background_art_is_pack_owned_and_irregular
```

### Owner correction: anchor Constellation lighting

### Owner-approved extension: bounded depth layers

Theme Pack v1 now accepts an optional ordered list of up to four sanitized SVG
background assets. Each declares only a depth factor from `0` (fixed viewport)
to `1` (full Board movement). The stable engine applies proportional movement;
an ordinary pack adds or changes layers using only its manifest and assets.
Runtime code contains no installed theme names.

Depth is decorative-only. Tiles, labels, focus surfaces, hit targets,
documents, and relationship connectors remain together at full movement, so
the Parent/Child Neighborhood is unchanged. Reduced-motion mode snaps layers
to their final proportional positions without transition.

Planets / Constellation is the only initial multi-depth theme. Its original
520 irregular stars are preserved but partitioned deterministically into a far
layer at `0.30` and a restrained near layer at `0.50`; fixed lighting remains at
`0`, while planets and their relationships remain at full Board movement.
Canonical, Lily, and Islands retain one layer at `1`. Cloudscape remains
disabled.

Focused tests cover strict bounds and the four-layer cap, sanitized ordered
assets, proportional motion at desktop and phone sizes, direct entry, theme
switch cleanup, reduced motion, unchanged single-plane themes, and the absence
of installed theme names in runtime source.

### Owner-approved extension: modular expanded actions

The expanded destination remains one semantic link in one fixed position, but
its visual Action Treatment is now pack-owned. `annotation` preserves the
Original paper behavior as writing directly on the sheet. `marker` gives worlds
whose solid objects do not naturally support handwriting a larger, opaque,
bordered in-world label. Marker actions settle at no less than 18-pixel type,
with a visible 38-pixel label and 44-pixel touch target on desktop and phone.

The stable engine and stylesheet know only the two treatment names. Color,
background, border, padding, radius, shadow, font, weight, decoration, and
transform remain inert Theme Pack data. Canonical selects `annotation` without
changing its existing presentation; Lily Pond, Planets / Constellation, and
Island Chain select `marker`. The destination, Parent/Child Neighborhood,
focus order, Enter/Space behavior, and document flow are unchanged.
