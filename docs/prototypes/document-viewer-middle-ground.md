# PROTOTYPE: Document Viewer middle ground

Question: Can alternate worlds keep one recognizable Portfolio Viewer while
moving strong world motifs out of the reading surface?

On any opened alternate-theme document, use the temporary bottom switcher or
the `docvariant` query parameter:

- `A` — Quiet canvas: familiar cards made from calm solid materials.
- `B` — Continuous page: one uninterrupted reading surface with section rules.
- `C` — Content plates: discrete solid artifacts with alternating edge rhythm.

All candidates preserve real content, document navigation, the outer Viewer,
and the Board. Canonical is excluded. This prototype branches from `0c15c3b`
and is intended to be replaced by the validated direction or reverted whole.
