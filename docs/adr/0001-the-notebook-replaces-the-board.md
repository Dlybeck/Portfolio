# The notebook replaces the board

The portfolio's interface is a notebook: a kraft worn cover with live vinyl stickers that
opens onto pages you turn with a side wheel. This replaces the earlier tile board (the
chalkboard, its map, chalk arrows and nine theme packs). Their history stays in git and in
the "This website" page; the board's rules and decision records are retired with it.

Why: the board was hard to move around on a phone, every theme multiplied the work of
keeping navigation identical, and its stories were split across small windows. The notebook
makes a page a page, so a story reads top to bottom and the wheel is the only thing to learn.

What stays from the board's spirit: the site is explored, not funneled. Every page is a real
link, and nothing about the notebook is required to read the words.

Design and content were reviewed as a clickable preview over several rounds (round 17 is
the approved design) before this was built.
