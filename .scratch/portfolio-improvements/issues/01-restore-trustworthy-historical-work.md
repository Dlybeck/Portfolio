# Restore trustworthy Historical Work

## Context

The portfolio is a living self-portrait, so its older generations and historical project material are first-class content rather than disposable résumé history. Several referenced images are currently missing, and a small set of labels, dates, identifiers, and copy contain mechanical errors. The goal is to restore trust without sanding away the author's voice or rewriting history.

## Requirements

- Repair the known incorrect image reference in the first-generation portfolio material.
- Attempt to recover the three missing third-generation images from reproducible historical repository states.
- If a missing historical image cannot be recovered confidently, stop and request owner review before removing it or substituting different content.
- Correct spelling mistakes, missing words, incorrect visible titles, duplicate identifiers, invalid markup, and inaccurate accessibility labels where the intended correction is mechanical and unambiguous.
- Record Technology Services as ending in May 2025.
- Verify the visible links within the affected historical material.
- Preserve the conversational voice, old coursework, historical framing, and on-page ScribbleScan performance claims.
- Do not infer or rewrite other dates, claims, tone, or history without owner review.
- Add repeatable validation for the classes of defect repaired by this ticket.

## Suggested approach

Inventory all locally referenced assets and malformed document structure first. Use repository history as evidence for image recovery and compare restored pages against their surrounding content. Keep mechanical corrections separate from editorial decisions so any ambiguity is easy to surface to the owner.

## Done when

- All intended local assets in the historical pages resolve, or each unresolved asset has an explicit owner decision.
- The confirmed Technology Services date and unambiguous mechanical corrections are present.
- A repeatable asset/reference and document-integrity check passes.
- Visible links in the affected material have been exercised.
- A manual desktop and phone review finds no unintended change in voice, layout, or historical meaning.
