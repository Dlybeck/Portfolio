# Personal Portfolio

A portrait of David Lybeck expressed through the things he has made, done, learned, and enjoyed, as a worn notebook you page through. It exists to be explored, not to deliver a prescribed professional pitch.

## Language

**Living Self-Portrait**:
The portfolio as a continuously evolving expression of David's personal, professional, educational, and recreational history.
_Avoid_: Resume site, hiring funnel, static archive

**Viewer**:
A person exploring the self-portrait, whether arriving with prior context or discovering it through search or a shared link. The viewer's ability to understand and enjoy the experience has priority.
_Avoid_: Recruiter, customer, user

**Notebook**:
The whole interface: a cover that opens onto pages you turn. On a phone it shows one page at a time; on desktop it is open to a two-page spread.
_Avoid_: Board, map, site shell

**Cover**:
The closed front of the Notebook, a kraft worn cover with live vinyl stickers. Opening it, or spinning the Wheel past page 1 and back, is how a Viewer enters and leaves.
_Avoid_: Landing page, splash screen

**Page**:
One story (ScribbleScan, Work, Tennis) written once in a template. A Page scrolls inside the Notebook; long stories are one Page, not several.
_Avoid_: Tile, panel, screen

**Spread**:
Two facing Pages on desktop. A story's Spread pairs its section's list of stories (or its first section) with the story itself.
_Avoid_: Layout, view

**Wheel**:
The side wheel with one notch per page and one for the Cover. Dragging, flicking, scrolling or using the keyboard turns the pages live. It is the main way around and never the only one.
_Avoid_: Menu, slider

**Contents**:
The list of what is in the Notebook, and the lists of stories at the front of Projects and Hobbies. Entries are real links.
_Avoid_: Sitemap, navbar

**Sticker**:
A live vinyl object on the Cover that does something small when touched (the name tag, the scanner pad, the tennis ball). Decoration with a point of view, not navigation.
_Avoid_: Button, widget

**Destination Link**:
A shareable address (any route in `core/portfolio.py`) that opens the Notebook at that Page, past the Cover.
_Avoid_: Standalone page, detached document

**Historical Work**:
A completed artifact retained because it contributes to the self-portrait, regardless of its age or current professional relevance. The earlier versions of this website are Historical Work.
_Avoid_: Obsolete content, low-signal work

**Artifact Label**:
Current explanatory copy, links, titles, and status information surrounding Historical Work. Labels are corrected as mistakes or stale facts are found without rewriting the underlying artifact's history.
_Avoid_: Historical artifact

**Preserved Demo**:
The publicly accessible demonstration of a former service, retained to show what the service did without representing an active business or service offering.
_Avoid_: Live service, current business

**Professional Discovery Layer**:
Search, social-preview, and machine-readable information that foregrounds David's current identity as an Innovation AI Developer and software builder while the visible self-portrait remains broader and personal.
_Avoid_: Professional homepage, recruiter funnel

**dlybeck design**:
David's personal design system (tokens, components, logos). The Notebook is drawn with it; colors, type, spacing and motion come from its tokens.
_Avoid_: Theme, skin
